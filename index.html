Privacy policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026  ·  Last updated: October 22, 2026

1. Introduction

DREAMFORGE STUDIO (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is a game development studio that develops and publishes games and related digital services.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and protect information when you play our games, visit our website, or interact with our services.

By using our games or services, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. Where consent is legally required, we will request it separately.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the game and the features you use, we may collect the following categories of information.

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Information you submit when contacting us for support or feedback.
  • Your chosen username, nickname, or other profile information, if supported by the game.
  • Information you provide when participating in surveys, promotions, or other optional activities.

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you use our games, certain information may be collected automatically, including:

  • Device model, operating system, and device type.
  • Game version, language, and general device settings.
  • Gameplay activity, progress, scores, achievements, and game preferences.
  • Crash reports, diagnostic information, and performance data.
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it, where processed by us or our service providers.
  • Advertising identifiers and related advertising information, where applicable.

The information collected depends on the specific game, your device settings, and the services integrated into that game.

2.3 Advertising and Analytics Information

Our games may use third-party advertising and analytics services to display advertisements, measure advertising performance, understand gameplay activity, and improve our games.

These services may collect device identifiers, advertising interactions, usage information, and other data according to their own privacy policies and applicable settings.

3. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Develop, operate, maintain, and improve our games.
  • Save game progress and provide leaderboards or other online features.
  • Provide customer support and respond to inquiries.
  • Diagnose crashes, fix bugs, and improve performance.
  • Display advertisements and measure advertising effectiveness.
  • Process or support in-app purchases through the relevant platform.
  • Detect fraud, abuse, cheating, and other activities that violate our terms.
  • Protect our users, services, and legal rights.
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulatory requirements.

We will use personal information only for appropriate purposes and in accordance with applicable law.

4. Third-Party Advertising and Mediation Services

DREAMFORGE STUDIO may use third-party advertising networks and advertising mediation platforms to display advertisements in its mobile games. Depending on the game, platform, region, and advertising configuration, these services may include:

4.1 AppLovin

We may use AppLovin advertising services and the AppLovin MAX mediation platform to display, optimize, and measure advertisements in our games.

AppLovin and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as:

  • Device information, including device model and operating system.
  • IP address and approximate location derived from network information.
  • Advertising identifiers and device identifiers, where available and permitted.
  • App usage, advertising interactions, and ad performance information.
  • Technical information used for fraud prevention, security, and advertising measurement.

This information may be used to deliver advertisements, personalize advertising where permitted, measure advertising effectiveness, and improve advertising services.

For more information, please review the AppLovin Privacy Policy.

4.2 Google AdMob

We may use Google AdMob to display advertisements, including banner ads, interstitial ads, and rewarded video ads.

Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as:

  • Device and advertising identifiers.
  • IP address and approximate location.
  • App interactions and advertising activity.
  • Diagnostic information and information used to measure advertising performance.
  • Information used to detect invalid traffic and prevent fraud.

Depending on your location, applicable consent requirements, and your device settings, this information may also be used to provide personalized or non-personalized advertisements.

For more information, please review the Google Privacy Policy and Google’s Advertising Policies and Privacy Information.

4.3 Unity Ads

We may use Unity Ads and related Unity advertising services to display advertisements in our games and measure their performance.

Unity and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as:

  • Device information and operating system details.
  • IP address and approximate location.
  • Advertising identifiers and other device identifiers, where available.
  • Information about ad impressions, clicks, views, and interactions.
  • Technical, diagnostic, and fraud-prevention information.

This information may be used to deliver advertisements, measure campaign performance, prevent fraud, and improve advertising services. Personalized advertising may be provided where permitted by applicable law and the user’s privacy choices.

For more information, please review the Unity Privacy Policy.

4.4 Advertising Mediation and Partner Networks

Where we use advertising mediation, including AppLovin MAX or another mediation platform, multiple advertising networks may participate in selecting and delivering advertisements.

Depending on the specific game and its configuration, these networks may include AppLovin, Google AdMob, Unity Ads, and other advertising partners.

As a result, information may be collected or processed by the mediation platform and participating advertising networks in accordance with their respective privacy policies. The networks used may vary between games and over time.

We do not claim that every advertising provider listed in this section is used in every game.

5. Advertising, Consent, and Privacy Choices

Our games may display banner advertisements, interstitial advertisements, rewarded video advertisements, and other supported advertising formats.

Advertising services may process information to:

  • Display and deliver advertisements.
  • Provide personalized or non-personalized advertising, where applicable.
  • Measure advertisement views and interactions.
  • Limit repeated advertisements.
  • Detect invalid traffic, fraud, and abuse.
  • Analyze and improve advertising performance.

5.1 Consent and Regional Requirements

Where required by applicable laws or platform policies, we will provide appropriate consent notices and privacy choices before permitting relevant data collection or personalized advertising.

These requirements may include applicable privacy laws and regulations in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, the United States, India, and other jurisdictions.

Where applicable, we will take reasonable steps to communicate users’ consent choices or restrictions to the relevant advertising and mediation partners.

5.2 Managing Advertising Preferences

Depending on your device and location, you may be able to manage advertising preferences through:

  • Your device’s advertising or privacy settings.
  • Consent options presented within the game.
  • Available privacy controls provided by Google, AppLovin, Unity, or other relevant advertising partners.

Disabling personalized advertising does not necessarily eliminate advertisements. You may continue to receive contextual or non-personalized advertisements.

5.3 Children’s Privacy and Advertising

We will configure advertising services and apply appropriate safeguards according to each game’s intended audience and applicable legal requirements.

Where a game is directed toward children or includes child users, we will apply the relevant restrictions and safeguards for data collection, advertising, consent, and advertising personalization. We will not knowingly permit advertising practices that violate applicable child privacy laws or platform policies.

5.4 Third-Party Responsibility

Third-party advertising providers may process information under their own privacy policies and applicable terms. DREAMFORGE STUDIO does not control every aspect of their independent data processing.

For details about how these providers collect, use, and share information, please consult their respective privacy policies linked above.

6. In-App Purchases and Payments

Some games may offer optional in-app purchases, virtual items, or other paid features.

Payments are generally processed by the applicable platform or payment provider, such as Google Play. We do not intentionally collect or store your complete payment card details through our games.

We may receive limited transaction-related information, such as purchase status or product identifiers, when necessary to verify purchases, provide purchased content, prevent fraud, or resolve support requests.

Payment processing is subject to the applicable platform’s terms and privacy policy.

7. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information in exchange for money.

We may disclose or make information available in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers: To companies that provide hosting, analytics, advertising, customer support, security, or other services necessary to operate our games.
  • Platform providers: To app stores and platform services that support installation, purchases, authentication, or gameplay features.
  • Legal requirements: When disclosure is required by law, legal process, or a valid request from an authorized authority.
  • Protection and security: When reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, protect users, enforce our terms, or safeguard our rights.
  • Business transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or transfer of relevant business assets, subject to applicable law.

Third-party advertising and analytics integrations may collect or share information directly under their own terms. Such processing will be handled in accordance with applicable privacy requirements.

8. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain gameplay features, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and protect our services.

Retention periods vary depending on the type of information, the game, the purpose of processing, and the requirements of third-party providers.

Where information is no longer needed, we will take reasonable steps to delete it or render it anonymous, subject to applicable law and legitimate retention requirements.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We therefore cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children’s Privacy

Our games are not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

The age suitability of each game may vary. Where a game is intended for children or is subject to child-specific privacy requirements, we will implement the applicable safeguards, consent mechanisms, advertising restrictions, and data-handling practices.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where parental consent is required but has not been obtained.

If you are a parent or legal guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us. We will review the request and take appropriate action in accordance with applicable law.

11. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of certain personal information.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Request a copy of certain information in a portable format.
  • Exercise applicable rights relating to targeted advertising or data sharing.

To exercise your rights, contact us using the details in the Contact Us section.

We may need to verify your identity before processing a request. Certain information may need to be retained where required or permitted by law.

You can also use the privacy controls available through your device, app store, or relevant third-party service.

12. International Data Transfers

Some of our service providers may process information on servers located outside your country of residence.

Where information is transferred internationally, we will take steps required by applicable law to ensure appropriate protection of that information.

Our games or website may contain links to third-party websites, applications, or services. We do not control the privacy practices of independent third parties.

Your use of those services is governed by their own terms and privacy policies. We recommend reviewing them before providing personal information.
